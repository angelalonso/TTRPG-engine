Honda South & Wales Trophy Sponsor

Apply for a Honda Civic EG loan deal covering the South & Wales Trophy. The sponsor provides the car and pays a result-based fee after each championship round.

  • 1st: £5,000
  • 2nd: £3,000
  • 3rd: £2,000
  • Other result: £500

You must be a member of the Honda Civic EG South & Wales Trophy. The sponsor's car is returned automatically at year end.
