South & Wales Trophy - Round 3

Mondello Park timed sprint

When
Day 164, 13 June 2026
Format
Closed timed sprint; optional overseas leg
Venue
Near Naas in County Kildare, Ireland. Mondello Park is Ireland's permanent FIA-licensed circuit; the International layout is about 2.250 miles and the National layout about 1.156 miles.
Calendar notes
Mild and typically wetter than mainland Britain. Estimated entry: 20-25 cars at 180-220 per driver including ferry costs.

The round is optional because it requires ferry travel. A suitable Irish licence and meeting booking would be required for wheel-to-wheel racing.
