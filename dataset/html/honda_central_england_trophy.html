Honda Civic EG Central England Trophy

The player's home-region trophy keeps the six rounds within a practical Midlands and southern England travel pattern. Silverstone, Mallory Park, Snetterton and Brands Hatch are race meetings; Rockingham and Bedford are private timed-sprint rounds.

  • Round 1, Day 95: Silverstone National
  • Round 2, Day 108: Mallory Park home round
  • Round 3, Day 129: Rockingham infield timed sprint
  • Round 4, Day 199: Bedford Autodrome timed sprint
  • Round 5, Day 214: Snetterton 300
  • Round 6, Day 243: Brands Hatch Indy finale

Rockingham is treated as conditional private access only: the calendar does not imply a return to normal public racing at the former speedway.
