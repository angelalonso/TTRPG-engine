Formation

Type: Venture

Commit to a two-year motorsport formation course to build your knowledge, contacts, and standing in the paddock.

What to expect

The course costs £50 per week and uses 14 stamina on each weekday. After 104 paid weeks, you graduate and receive 100 Paddock Cred.

Requirements and preparation

Formation is a Venture, so it can be pursued alongside a part-time job. Missing too many formation days ends the course. Keep enough budget for the weekly fee and enough stamina for weekday study.
