Silverstone Open Race

Type: Open race

Silverstone Open Race is an open-entry race for drivers who want a straightforward result at the circuit. It is a chance to earn prize money and build racing reputation without joining a championship series.

What to expect

The race takes 1 day. Entry costs £850; the prize pool is £6,500, with a listed charisma reward of 4. Any owned vehicle is eligible, subject to the normal licence and car-condition checks.

If you do not own a suitable car, you can rent any vehicle from the dataset for the day. The rental costs one twenty-fifth of its listed price; your racing licence and race gear are still required, while the rental covers damage and pre-race service costs.

Requirements and preparation

This entry requires the license basic. Bring a serviced car, confirm that you hold the required racing licence, and leave enough stamina and budget for the rest of the day.
