Brands Hatch Track Day

Type: Track day

Brands Hatch Track Day is a non-competitive circuit session. Use it to get meaningful seat time, learn the venue and prepare a car before committing to a race weekend.

What to expect

The session lasts 6 hours and costs £300 to enter. Any owned vehicle can be selected for a track day; the car still needs to be available and free of blocking mechanical service.

If you do not own a suitable car, you can rent any vehicle from the dataset for the session. The rental costs one twenty-fifth of its listed price; your racing licence and race gear are still required, while the rental covers damage and pre-race service costs.

Requirements and preparation

This entry requires no special entry requirement beyond the normal activity rules. Check the car's service status before booking. Track time consumes the race-day stamina allowance, and the tyres will need attention after the session.
