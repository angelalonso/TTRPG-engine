Temporary Job

Type: Job

Temporary Job is a way to turn time and stamina into dependable income while your racing career develops. The result is resolved as a work outcome rather than a race result.

What to expect

Starting this job costs £0 and uses 3 stamina. Its configured success chance is 95%, and the payout is £11 per day when applicable.

Requirements and preparation

This entry requires no special entry requirement beyond the normal activity rules. Use ordinary work to build funds between race events. Keep an eye on stamina and avoid scheduling it when you need to be fresh for a race or another time-sensitive activity.
