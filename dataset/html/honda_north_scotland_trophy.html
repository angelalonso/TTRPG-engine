Honda Civic EG North & Scotland Trophy

A six-round regional trophy for Honda Civic EG drivers across northern England and Scotland. Croft, Aintree, Cadwell Park, Donington and Oulton offer conventional club racing; the Aintree round is a closed timed sprint. The season finale is a Knockhill day/night-style meeting.

  • Round 1, Day 101: Croft Circuit
  • Round 2, Day 115: Aintree timed sprint
  • Round 3, Day 124: Cadwell Park
  • Round 4, Day 145: Donington Park National
  • Round 5, Day 185: Oulton Park
  • Round 6, Day 269: Knockhill finale

Points accumulate across all six rounds. The trophy is a hypothetical regional grouping based on the 2026 calendar concept.
