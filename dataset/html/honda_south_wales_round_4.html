Format
Wheel-to-wheel club race, approximately 12 laps
Venue
Near Andover, Hampshire. The former RAF airfield circuit is 2.356 miles long and is known for fast sweeps, high average speed and heavy tyre loads.

Warm, exposed and windy summer conditions. Estimated field: 15-20 cars; estimated attendance: 2,000-3,000.

Thruxton is the trophy's high-speed test: drivers need confidence, clean lines and careful tyre management.
