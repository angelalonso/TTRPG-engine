Formula Ford Sponsor and Open Day

A sponsor-facing paddock day built around the accessible, low-downforce Formula Ford scene. Demonstration runs, photographs and short conversations with prospective partners are more important than lap times.

When
Day 45
Format
Non-race social and sponsor event
Car required
Formula Ford 1600
Reward

Formula Ford is known for close, relatively simple single-seater racing, making it an approachable platform for a first sponsor presentation.
