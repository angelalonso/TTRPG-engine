North & Scotland Trophy - Round 2

Aintree Circuit timed sprint

When
Day 115, 25 April 2026
Format
Closed timed sprint; no lap-count race
Venue
Merseyside, Liverpool. The surviving club circuit is about 1.370 miles long and sits beside the historic Aintree racecourse.
Calendar notes
Mild, breezy conditions are expected. The circuit is associated with motorcycle racing, sprints, testing and track activity, so this Honda round is deliberately private rather than a public race meeting.

Drivers are ranked by sprint time for championship points. Estimated entry: 20-25 cars, approximately 140-160 per driver.
