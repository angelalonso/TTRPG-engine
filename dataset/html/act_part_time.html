Part-time job

Type: Job

A part-time job provides a smaller but steadier income stream while leaving more energy for racing.

What to expect

The job pays £125 each week when the weekly payroll succeeds. It uses 14 stamina on each weekday, half the weekday stamina requirement of the standard 9-5 job.

Requirements and preparation

You can only hold one job at a time. Keep enough stamina for each weekday and remember that missed workdays can eventually end the job.
