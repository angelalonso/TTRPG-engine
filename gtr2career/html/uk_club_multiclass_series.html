UK Club Multi-Class Cup

This championship is a season-long series of scheduled races. Join before entering its rounds, then record each result to build championship points.

Championship rounds

  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 1: Brands Hatch
  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 2: Donington Park
  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 3: Oulton Park
  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 4: Thruxton
  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 5: Silverstone
  • UK Club Multi-Class Cup - Round 6: Croft

Entry and rewards

The championship entry fee is £350. Final championship rewards are paid by finishing position according to the configured championship table.

Each round has its own entry requirements, entry fee, and race-day result. Check the race page for the specific circuit and preparation details.
