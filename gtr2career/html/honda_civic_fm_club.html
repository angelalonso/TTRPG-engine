Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series

This championship is a season-long series of scheduled races. Join before entering its rounds, then record each result to build championship points.

Championship rounds

  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 1: Mallory Park
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 2: Pembrey
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 3: Bedford Autodrome (SEN)
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 4: Mondello Park (National)
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 5: Aintree
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 6: Castle Combe
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - Club Series - Round 7: Oulton Park- Finale

Entry and rewards

The championship entry fee is £250. Final championship rewards are paid by finishing position according to the configured championship table.

Each round has its own entry requirements, entry fee, and race-day result. Check the race page for the specific circuit and preparation details.
