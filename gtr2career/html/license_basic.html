Motorsport UK National B (Race) Licence

Purpose & Difference

The entry point to competitive circuit racing. It allows drivers with zero prior racing experience to compete in domestic, entry-level UK club events such as the Mazda MX-5 Championship, Caterhams, and regional Formula Ford.

Requirements

Acquire the required race gear before applying.

Licence details

  • Licence level: National B
  • Fee: £500
