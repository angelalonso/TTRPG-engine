South Wales Amateur Circuit Series

This championship is a season-long series of scheduled races. Join before entering its rounds, then record each result to build championship points.

Championship rounds

  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 1: Pembrey
  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 2: Castle Combe
  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 3: Anglesey
  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 4: Oulton Park
  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 5: Thruxton
  • South Wales Amateur Circuit Series - Round 6: Mallory Park

Entry and rewards

The championship entry fee is £200. Final championship rewards are paid by finishing position according to the configured championship table.

Each round has its own entry requirements, entry fee, and race-day result. Check the race page for the specific circuit and preparation details.
