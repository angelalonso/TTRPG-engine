Full insurance (monthly)

Covers damage to your vehicle with the full policy limit. There is no upfront insurance fee; the calculated annual premium is charged in twelve equal monthly payments. The premium starts at £750 plus 5% of the value of all owned cars, capped at £30,000 per year.
