Third-party insurance (monthly)

Only pays for damage to other vehicles. There is no upfront insurance fee; the calculated annual premium is charged in twelve equal monthly payments. The premium starts at £250 plus 1.5% of the value of all owned cars, capped at £2,000 per year.
