Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series

This championship is a season-long series of scheduled races. Join before entering its rounds, then record each result to build championship points.

Championship rounds

  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 1: Castle Combe
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 2: Thruxton
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 3: Cadwell Park
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 4: Snetterton (200)
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 5: Croft
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 6: Anglesey (Coastal)
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 7: Mondello Park (International)
  • Honda Civic FM Championship - National Series - Round 8: Brands Hatch (Indy)- Finale

Entry and rewards

The championship entry fee is £250. Final championship rewards are paid by finishing position according to the configured championship table.

Each round has its own entry requirements, entry fee, and race-day result. Check the race page for the specific circuit and preparation details.
