UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series

This championship is a season-long series of scheduled races. Join before entering its rounds, then record each result to build championship points.

Championship rounds

  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 1: Mallory Park
  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 2: Bedford Autodrome
  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 3: Cadwell Park
  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 4: Anglesey
  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 5: Castle Combe
  • UK Hot Hatch Championship - Club Festival Series - Round 6: Pembrey- Finale

Entry and rewards

The championship entry fee is £250. Final championship rewards are paid by finishing position according to the configured championship table.

Each round has its own entry requirements, entry fee, and race-day result. Check the race page for the specific circuit and preparation details.
