Motorsport UK National A (Race) Licence

Purpose & Difference

Marks the step up to premier UK-wide racing. It proves basic racecraft, track safety, and clean finishes. This licence unlocks higher-tier national championships, faster machinery, and flagship UK professional series such as BTCC / British Touring Cars.

Requirements

The National B Race Licence must already be held before this licence can be acquired.

Licence details

  • Licence level: National A
  • Fee: £1,200
