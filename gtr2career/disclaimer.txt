ABOUT THE GAME

- This is a proof of concept, and yes, I used AI extensively to code it. The content is all my fault and not the machine's.

- There is a dataset editor, if you feel something is off, improve it and share it. 

- Unfortunately, as long as I keep modifying the game, every new version has incompatibilities with previous ones. This messes with save files, and I have tried to put together a migration system, and leave the player the option to use the save file anyways. Sorry about that, there is no solution yet.

ABOUT THE MODS

- I have created None of the mods. All credit goes to their respective creators.

- I have created some modifications that you are free to use or not. My intention was to make the Mods fit the story a bit better (e.g.: that a car of a lower category should be slower than that of a higher one)

- Regarding my choices of mods, my goal was to put together a story, even the simplest one. I decided to avoid historical accuracy and use the cars that I found most interesting and fitting. 
This means you race a 2010 FWD car, then progress into a 1998 BTCC championship. 

- I dropped the natural karting starter pack because I dont find karts in GTR2 are good enough, so I went for amateur cars as an entry level instead.

- Some things are not realistic enough, and I would like to go for that, but my testing showed that the complexity to have things like a "used car market" that evolves and changes constantly is way too big.
