# Honda Civic EG – UK Amateur Racing Calendar 2026 (v3: regional championships)
### Three 6-round regional trophies, grouped by geography to cut travel time — plus Rockingham, honestly included

**Base year:** 2026 (not a leap year, 365 days, Day 1 = Thursday 1 January)
**Home track:** Mallory Park, Kirkby Mallory, Leicestershire

---

## Read this first: how "regional championships" actually works here

**Real UK club championships don't split by region** — Racing Hondas, CSCC, and 750 Motor Club (the three real series behind the previous version of this calendar) are all national by design, because touring the whole country is what makes the entry fees and prize pool viable. There's no real precedent for a genuinely regional single-make Civic series.

So what you have below is an honest **hypothetical restructuring**: I've taken the real 2026 dates, venues, pricing and attendance patterns from Racing Hondas/CSCC/750 Motor Club (already verified in the previous version) and **regrouped them geographically** into three fictional 6-round regional trophies. The venues, dates, and event "flavour" are as realistic as I could make them — but the specific championship branding ("North & Scotland Trophy," etc.) is invented for your stated goal of less travel, not a real thing you'll find advertised anywhere. Where I've reused a real date, I've noted which actual series really races there on that day.

## Rockingham — how it's included

Rockingham Motor Speedway's banked oval and grandstands closed permanently in 2018 and the site is now mostly a vehicle logistics/storage park — that part hasn't changed. **However**, the tarmac of its old infield "International" road course still physically exists, and the venue has hosted limited trackday activity since closure (a motorcycle trackday operator reopened part of it in 2019, and the site still sees occasional private test/track use). So Rockingham appears here as a **closed, private trackday/timed-sprint round** — no public gate, no grandstand crowd — rather than a fictional revival of full wheel-to-wheel racing, which genuinely hasn't happened there since 2018.

## A note on race format

Not every venue below can host a real wheel-to-wheel race in 2026. Aintree, Rockingham, Bedford Autodrome, Anglesey, and Mondello Park don't currently have (or ever had, in Mondello's case for this car) a confirmed Honda-relevant race meeting, so those rounds are run as **closed, timed sprint/trackday format** — same championship points, no public spectators. Everywhere else is a genuine public race meeting, using real dates lifted from Racing Hondas, CSCC, or 750 Motor Club's actual 2026 calendars.

## The Three Regional Trophies

| Trophy | Region | Rounds | Why this grouping |
|---|---|---|---|
| **North & Scotland Trophy** | Scotland, North England, North Midlands | Croft → Aintree → Cadwell Park → Donington Park → Oulton Park → Knockhill | Keeps travel within the north; only the Knockhill finale demands a long haul, same as it would for any real northern-based series |
| **Central England Trophy** | East/West Midlands, home region | Silverstone → **Mallory Park** → Rockingham → Bedford → Snetterton → Brands Hatch | The player's home trophy — every round is within ~2.5 hours of Leicestershire |
| **South & Wales Trophy** | South England, Wales, Ireland | Anglesey → Pembrey → Mondello Park (Ireland) → Thruxton → Goodwood → Castle Combe | South/West-leaning, with Mondello as a genuinely optional overseas leg rather than a mandatory long-haul round |
