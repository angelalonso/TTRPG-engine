GT and Supercar Media Day

A press and photography day for a serious GT or N-GT machine. The car is available for controlled static photography, technical interviews and short demonstration sessions for motoring media.

When
Day 180
Format
Non-race media and demonstration event
Eligible cars
FIA GT, FIA N-GT or Porsche Cup
Reward
+4 charisma if the event goes well

Three eligible cars can enter this event, but only one is needed. The media wants a good story, a clean car and a driver who can stop talking before the camera battery dies.

Event concept informed by FIA circuit-racing media activity and manufacturer track demonstrations.
