Honda North & Scotland Trophy Sponsor

Apply for a championship-only Honda Civic EG sponsorship. If accepted, the sponsor loans you the car for the current year and pays after each round according to your result.

  • 1st: £5,000
  • 2nd: £3,000
  • 3rd: £2,000
  • Other result: £500

You must join the Honda Civic EG North & Scotland Trophy first. The loaned car returns to the sponsor at the end of the year.
