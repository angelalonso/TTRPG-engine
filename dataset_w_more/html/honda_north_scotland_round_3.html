Cadwell Park

When
Day 124, 4 May 2026
Format
Wheel-to-wheel club race, approximately 9 laps
Venue
Near Louth, Lincolnshire. The 2.176-mile full car layout is narrow, undulating and often described as a roller-coaster through the countryside.
Calendar notes
A mild bank-holiday meeting with sunny intervals and a breeze. Estimated field: 15-20 cars; estimated attendance: 1,500-3,000.

The narrow track makes clean positioning and commitment through the rises more valuable than outright top speed.
