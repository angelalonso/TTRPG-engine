Hot Hatch Enthusiasts Meet

A relaxed owners' meet for the hot-hatch paddock. Any of the eligible hatchbacks can represent the cup, with a static display, a technical Q&A and a short parade lap if the venue allows it.

When
Day 205
Format
Non-race owners' meet and parade event
Eligible cars
Alfa Romeo MiTo, Citroen DS3, Ford Fiesta, Seat Ibiza, Vauxhall Corsa, Peugeot 207, Toyota Yaris or Renault Clio
Reward
+3 charisma if the event goes well

There are enough badges, trim levels and strong opinions here to fill an entire afternoon.

Event concept informed by club motorsport and hot-hatch owners' meet formats.
