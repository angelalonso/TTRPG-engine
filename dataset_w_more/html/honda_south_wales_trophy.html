Honda Civic EG South & Wales Trophy

A south-western regional trophy with one optional Irish leg. The calendar uses closed timed sprints at Anglesey, Mondello Park and Goodwood, while Pembrey, Thruxton and Castle Combe host conventional club-race rounds.

  • Round 1, Day 80: Anglesey timed sprint
  • Round 2, Day 142: Pembrey Circuit
  • Round 3, Day 164: Mondello Park timed sprint
  • Round 4, Day 227: Thruxton
  • Round 5, Day 276: Goodwood timed sprint
  • Round 6, Day 290: Castle Combe finale

Mondello Park is an optional overseas round requiring ferry travel. Goodwood is framed as a fringe sprint alongside a major show rather than an invented standalone public race.
