Touring Car Charity Parade

A public-facing charity parade that puts a classic BTCC Super Touring car and Honda Civic EG side by side. Fans get photographs, signed programme cards and a slow demonstration lap.

When
Day 320
Format
Non-race charity parade and fan engagement event
Eligible cars
BTCC Super Touring Car (1998) or Honda Civic Cup (EG9)
Reward
+5 charisma if the event goes well

The goal is to leave the crowd talking about the cars and the cause. Overtaking the parade marshal is specifically not part of the programme.

Event concept informed by BTCC fan engagement and motorsport charity parades.
