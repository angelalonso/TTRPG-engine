Caterham Lightweight Festival

A club-style gathering celebrating lightweight sports cars. The Caterham is displayed in the paddock, used for passenger rides and discussed with owners who care about low weight, simple engineering and corner speed.

When
Day 150
Format
Non-race marque festival and passenger-ride event
Car required
Caterham Seven 320
Reward
+3 charisma if the event goes well

Passenger rides are tightly controlled: enthusiasm is welcome, but explaining the difference between a demo lap and a race start is essential.

Event concept informed by Caterham Cars owner and track-day activities.
