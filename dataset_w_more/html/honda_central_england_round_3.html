Central England Trophy - Round 3

Rockingham infield timed sprint

When
Day 129, 9 May 2026
Format
Closed timed sprint; no lap-count race
Venue
Corby, Northamptonshire. The former oval and road-course complex includes a long International configuration of about 2.560 miles.
Calendar notes
The site is treated as private/conditional access because public racing ended after the speedway closed. Estimated entry: 20-25 cars at 150-170 per driver.

The round uses the surviving infield tarmac as a timed test, not as a claim that normal public wheel-to-wheel racing has returned.
