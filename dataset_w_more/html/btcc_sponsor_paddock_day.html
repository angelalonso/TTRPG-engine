BTCC Sponsor Paddock Day

A hospitality and media day for a classic BTCC Super Touring car. Guests get a guided paddock walk, sponsor photographs and a carefully supervised demonstration before the car is displayed for interviews.

When
Day 72
Format
Non-race sponsor, hospitality and media event
Car required
BTCC Super Touring Car (1998)
Reward
+4 charisma if the event goes well

BTCC meetings are built around racing, paddock access and fan engagement, so a sponsor day fits the touring-car world without adding another race.

Event concept informed by the British Touring Car Championship paddock and hospitality format.
