High-Risk Crypto Trade

Type: Trade or investment

High-Risk Crypto Trade is a higher-variance money-making opportunity. It can improve the budget quickly, but the result is deliberately less reliable than regular work.

What to expect

The activity requires £500 up front, uses 2 stamina and has a configured success chance of 5%. A successful result pays up to £5,000.

Requirements and preparation

This entry requires no special entry requirement beyond the normal activity rules. Only commit money you can afford to leave at risk. A failed result can be more expensive than a normal work shift.
