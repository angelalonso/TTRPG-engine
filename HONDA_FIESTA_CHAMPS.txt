**A few realism notes:**
- **Rockingham** actually closed to circuit racing years ago and has since been redeveloped, so a real championship couldn't use it today — I kept it in as a nostalgic one-off since you listed it, but you may want to swap it for a repeat visit to Silverstone or Snetterton if you want strict current-day accuracy.
- **Goodwood** rarely hosts regular club championship rounds due to noise restrictions (it's mostly Members' Meeting/Revival/track days), so I used it as a single sprint-format club finale rather than a full double-header — that's how it tends to appear in real amateur calendars.
- **Mondello Park** is in Ireland, not the UK, but plenty of British club championships (e.g. some 750 Motor Club series) do run one "away round" there each year, so I used it that way for the Fiesta club series.
- Every national round assumes the standard **two-race weekend** format (Saturday qualifying + Race 1, Sunday Race 2); club rounds are shown as single-day since many smaller series run just one longer race per meeting to save costs.
