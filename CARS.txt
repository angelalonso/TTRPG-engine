VW Scirocco
The engine makes some weird pops and bangs, but at least it’s the best bang for your buck. 

With this 80s beauty, finishing anywhere other than last is a win.

Ford Fiesta ST
A chav’s wet dream, which explains why you can actually afford one now. 

Short gearing and a stiff rear axle mean this thing genuinely rotates.

Honda Civic
A proper JDM lunatic. The engine is lazy down low, so you’ll want to keep it high-revved and screaming for as long as you can. 

In 20 years, this thing will probably sell for NSX money.

Fiat 500 Abarth
If it sounds like an angry Ferrari, smells like an angry Ferrari, and looks like a cute restomod, it can only be Italian. 

It’s one of the most modern cars you’ll see at a local track day, and it’s a total weapon.

HotHatch 200 Category:
Alfa Romeo MiTo, Citroën DS3, Ford Fiesta, SEAT Ibiza, Vauxhall Corsa, Peugeot 207, Toyota Yaris, Renault Clio

This car is both pumped up and capped down at 200 hp and 1,000 kg to compete in the HotHatch 200 category. 

Don’t get too attached—this is NOT the HotHatch 300 class that could land you a drive in the BTCC. 

Still, enjoy the process and learn the ropes while rubbing doors with the other wannabes.
